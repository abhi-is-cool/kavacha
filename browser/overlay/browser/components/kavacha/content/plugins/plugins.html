Plugins

Extend Kavacha with small, permissioned add-ons. A plugin can only do what you explicitly allow — and can never touch your passwords, history, or private data.

Plugins are sideloaded into your profile at kavacha-plugins/. Drop a plugin folder there, then enable it below. Installing plugins from the Marketplace is a later integration.
